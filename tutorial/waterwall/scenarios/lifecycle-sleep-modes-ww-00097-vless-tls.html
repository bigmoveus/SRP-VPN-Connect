On Sleep: Stay, Pause and Off

Checks what each On Sleep choice does when the phone sleeps and wakes, together with Auto Reconnect.

What the test does

  1. The test runs four VPN sessions, one after another: Stay with Auto Reconnect on, Pause with Auto Reconnect off, Off with Auto Reconnect on, and Off with Auto Reconnect off.
  2. In each session it connects, tells the tunnel the phone is going to sleep, and checks that Stay keeps the connection while Pause and Off close it.
  3. It then tells the tunnel the phone woke up.
  4. For the first three sessions the connection must come back within 30 seconds and pass the app's HTTPS check.
  5. For Off without Auto Reconnect, the VPN must end within 15 seconds with the message "The connection was closed while the device slept."

Passes when all four sessions behave as described.

Gaps and limits

  • The phone did not really sleep, and WaterWall inside the app did not really crash: a Debug build of the app accepts test commands that make the tunnel act as if it had. Stopping the server is real.
  • Each step ran once, on one phone and one network.
