What the test does

  1. The profile uses On Sleep Pause, Auto Reconnect on and Retry Times 2.
  2. The test connects, tells the tunnel the phone is going to sleep, and checks that the connection closes.
  3. It tells the tunnel the phone woke up; the connection must be back within 25 seconds.
  4. It runs downloads and a UDP echo exchange through the tunnel for 90 seconds.
  5. It makes WaterWall inside the app stop; Auto Reconnect must bring the connection back within 30 seconds.
  6. It runs the downloads and the UDP exchange for another 90 seconds and disconnects.

Passes when the connection comes back both times and the downloads and the UDP exchange succeed in both phases.

Gaps and limits

  • The phone did not really sleep, and WaterWall inside the app did not really crash: a Debug build of the app accepts test commands that make the tunnel act as if it had. Stopping the server is real.
  • Each step ran once, on one phone and one network.
